# Decision 0001: Position MirrorShop as an AI storefront visibility audit

Date: 2026-04-21

We considered leading with `llms.txt` generation, but chose to position MirrorShop as an AI storefront visibility audit because the memorable product insight is the gap between what the merchant intends and what an AI shopping agent can infer from public storefront content.

`llms.txt` remains an output artifact, not the headline.
